# Colour Palette Bank

Use this as a reference library of colour palettes. When I ask for a design, suggest the best-fitting palette from this bank (or explain why none fits), and use the exact hex codes below.

## How to use these palettes
- **60/30/10 rule:** 60% dominant (backgrounds), 30% secondary (text, large blocks), 10% accent (buttons, highlights). Four-colour palettes add a small **support** colour, which gives roughly 60/25/10/5.
- **The accent is often already in the product.** Take the accent from the hero image or subject (the product, food, object), then build calm base colours around it.
- **Reserve reward colours.** If a colour is marked for rewards, use it only for stars, badges and celebrations.
- **Accessibility:** body text must reach at least 4.5:1 contrast with its background. Bright accents often need a deeper shade behind white text.
- **Avoid my default:** don't use cream, sage, forest green and gold unless I ask. That combination already appears in too many of my projects.

## Palettes in detail

### 1. Late Sets
- **Mood:** Jazz club, risograph print, nightlife, editorial
- **Inspired by:** Basement Seven: 'Late Sets' landing page
- **Dominant (60%):** Cream Paper `#F8DBC1`
- **Secondary (30%):** Riso Teal `#1D646C`
- **Accent (10%):** Fluorescent Pink `#EA4F88`
- **Ratio in the reference design:** 60-40 / 40-40-20 (teal headline + info block and large pink hero image push teal and pink well past 30/10)
- **Tips:** Grain/halftone textures suit it. To hit true 60/30/10, shrink the pink hero image or render it duotone teal with pink only on the CTA.

### 2. Fired Twice
- **Mood:** Ceramics, craft, earthy, calm luxury
- **Inspired by:** Atelier Ruo: 'Fired twice' landing page
- **Dominant (60%):** Raw Clay `#D0BCAC`
- **Secondary (30%):** Iron Brown `#461C0C`
- **Accent (10%):** Turquoise `#269991`
- **Ratio in the reference design:** 80-20 overall, 60-30-10 by role (clay background dominates; brown is mostly type; turquoise only in the glaze)
- **Tips:** Brown works best as text/linework rather than fills. Keep turquoise rare: it should feel like the glaze.

### 3. Ribbon Finals
- **Mood:** Sport, performance, premium events, bold contrast
- **Inspired by:** Ritmo Lisboa: 'Ribbon Finals' landing page
- **Dominant (60%):** Petrol Blue `#002D37`
- **Secondary (30%):** Ivory `#FEF0DD`
- **Accent (10%):** Saffron Gold `#F7A724`
- **Ratio in the reference design:** 70-30 / 60-30-10: the cleanest application of the rule of the three
- **Tips:** Dark-mode friendly. Ivory for headlines and nav, gold for motion/hero accents and primary CTA.

### 4. Drift
- **Mood:** Aquarium, underwater, nighttime glow, immersive
- **Inspired by:** Lumen Aquarium: 'Drift with the jellies'
- **Dominant (60%):** Deep Sea Blue `#012438`
- **Secondary (30%):** Foam White `#DBF4FC`
- **Accent (10%):** Sunset Orange `#F16F1B`
- **Ratio in the reference design:** 60-30-10 by role; the glowing jellyfish pushes orange towards ~20%
- **Tips:** Orange glows against the deep blue. Use it for the script headline word and glow effects; foam white for body type and outline buttons.

### 5. Zesty
- **Mood:** Drinks, FMCG, playful, loud pop
- **Inspired by:** Citra: 'ZESTY' sparkling lemonade page
- **Dominant (60%):** Lemon Yellow `#FCDA25`
- **Secondary (30%):** Electric Lavender `#954BF6`
- **Accent (10%):** Charcoal `#2A272C`
- **Ratio in the reference design:** 60-30-10, but here lavender is the 30 and charcoal is the 10 (the reverse of the swatch order)
- **Tips:** The can sets the accent. Lavender for the big type, can and CTA circle; charcoal only for nav, small labels and one word of the headline.

### 6. Hanare

### 13. Jollof Kitchen
- **Best for:** Meal planning / home wellbeing / habit apps
- **Mood:** Warm, appetising, calm base with a joyful reward pop; ADHD-friendly
- **Inspired by:** Recommended for Supperfold-style apps (formerly Project Phoenix) (meal plan, checklist, streaks, rewards). Deliberately avoids the sage/cream/gold default.
- **Dominant (60%):** Rice Paper `#FAF6F1`. App background; cards are #FFFFFF; lines #ECE4DC
- **Secondary (30%):** Aubergine Ink `#2B1E33`. Text, headings, floating nav pill; Aubergine #4A2F5C for brand fills; soft text #6B5E73
- **Accent (10%):** Jollof `#E0522B`. Completion ring, active tab, highlights. For buttons and links with text use Jollof Deep #C2401C (AA 5.2:1 with white)
- **Support (~5%):** Cucumber `#23766B`. Done/success states, ticked boxes, 'Ready now'; tint #DDEFEA
- **Reward only:** Plantain `#F2B33A`. Stars, badges and level-ups ONLY, always with ink text (8.3:1); tint #FCEBC4
- **Dark mode:** background `#1C1422`, card `#271D2E`, text `#F3ECF5`, text soft `#B8AABF`, jollof `#F06A43`, cucumber `#4FB3A4`, plantain `#F5BD52`
- **Status dots:** quick `#23766B`, medium `#D4892A`, special `#C2401C` (always pair the dot with a text label)
- **Contrast checked:** ink on paper 14.6:1, soft text on paper 5.6:1, white on jollof deep 5.2:1, white on cucumber 5.4:1, ink on plantain 8.3:1
- **Tips:** Draws its accent from her actual food (jollof, pepper soup, plantain, cucumber, kimchi). Warm reds and oranges stimulate appetite; aubergine is calm and grounded without being green; teal success reads as 'fresh'. The reward yellow is reserved so stars still feel special.
