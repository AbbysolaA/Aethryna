Build a dashboard called Application Desk, using the Evidence Desk design system and the five files in the tracker folder. It must answer one question: which application should I work on next, and what stops me applying?

Show these sections, in this order:
1. Headline figures. Include essential coverage, defined as: covered = 1, partial = 0.5, gap = 0, averaged across the Essential requirements only. Also show open gaps, claims checked and statement word count.
2. The pipeline by stage.
3. The requirements, each with its status.
4. The claims, each with its evidence chips.
5. The gap callouts, each with its next step.
6. A chart of used and unused evidence lines.

Do not use words that predict success. Show what is supported and what is not.
