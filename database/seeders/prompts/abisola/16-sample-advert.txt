# Programme Manager, Service Transformation (sample advert)

Northvale Council (fictional) is a local authority improving the services its residents use every day. We are recruiting a Programme Manager to lead a programme of service transformation projects across the council, linking the corporate plan to the work services do day to day.

Permanent, full time, hybrid working. Salary £48,000 to £53,000. Closing date (sample): 30 November 2026.

## About the role
You will lead the programme and its projects, line manage a small team of project managers, and work with HR, Finance, Communications and service leads to turn business needs into measurable outcomes. You will report progress and risks to the programme sponsor and to senior officers and elected members.

## You will
- Lead the programme and its projects from scoping through delivery to benefits review.
- Manage risks, issues and dependencies, and escalate significant ones to the sponsor.
- Line manage a team of project managers and co-ordinate their work.
- Work with HR, Finance, Communications and service leads to deliver outcomes.
- Report progress, benefits and risks to senior officers and elected members.
- Set and apply programme standards across the council.
- Manage the programme budget.

## Essential
- Programme management experience across the full lifecycle, with a record of turning business requirements into measurable outcomes.
- A recognised programme or project management qualification, or equivalent experience.
- Experience of running programmes within a local authority.
- Experience of line managing or leading a team of project managers.
- Experience of reporting to senior leaders and elected members.
- Experience of managing a programme budget.

## Desirable
- Knowledge of the challenges facing public services.
- Experience of designing programme standards or a programme office.
- Experience of using data and dashboards to report benefits.
